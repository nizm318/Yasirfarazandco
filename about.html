Who we are

We interpret the numbers, not just process them.

Yasir Faraz and Co. is a leading Chartered Accountancy firm dedicated to providing comprehensive financial solutions, serving as a trusted advisor to clients ranging from early-stage startups to established multinational corporations.

Our team brings deep-seated expertise in Statutory Audit, Strategic Tax Planning, and Corporate Advisory. We don't just process numbers — we interpret them, helping you navigate the complexities of the financial landscape while staying compliant, resilient, and ready for what's next.

We are a tech-forward CA firm that combines traditional values with modern efficiency, leveraging FinTech and Cloud Accounting to give business leaders real-time financial clarity. We specialise in GST Optimization, Startup Fundraising, International Taxation, and Audit & Assurance.
