04 / Taxation & Advisory

Income Tax Services

FY 2025 · Income Tax Act, 2025

In the Indian landscape of 2025, compliance has shifted from manual filings to a highly automated, data-driven environment — no longer a year-end activity, but a continuous monthly and quarterly cycle. We work across two lanes: staying compliant, and defending your position when disputes arise.

Lane 01 / Staying Compliant

Corporate & Individual Filings

Timely computation, advance tax planning, and seamless filing of ITR-1 through ITR-7 with complete disclosure accuracy.

TDS, TCS & Periodic Reporting

End-to-end quarterly returns, AIS/TIS and Form 26AS real-time reconciliations, and lower-deduction certificate filings.

Transfer Pricing & Form 3CEB

Benchmark study documentation, cross-border related-party transaction audits, and Specified Financial Transaction (SFT) filings.

Lane 02 / Defending Your Position

Faceless Assessments & Scrutiny

Drafting structured, evidence-backed submissions for notices under Section 142(1), 143(2), and reassessments under Section 148.

Appellate Representation & Litigation

Preparing Grounds of Appeal, Statement of Facts, and representation before the Commissioner of Income Tax (Appeals) / ITAT.

Rectifications & Demand Resolution

Handling Section 154 rectification requests, outstanding tax demand cancellation, penalty waivers, and compounding applications.
