Lead — Business Advisory & Corporate Laws

CA Yasir Faraz, FAFD (ICAI), IND AS (ICAI), AI (ICAI)

CA Yasir Faraz is the visionary founder and proprietor of Yasir Faraz and Co., bringing a distinguished background of experience in audit, taxation, and strategic financial advisory. A Member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), he established the firm with a steadfast commitment to integrity and precision.

Under his leadership, the firm has evolved from a boutique practice into a trusted partner for numerous clients across diverse financial disciplines. Known for a hands-on approach, Yasir Faraz specializes in navigating complex regulatory landscapes, ensuring that clients don't just meet compliance standards but achieve sustainable growth.

To deliver future-ready financial leadership, he integrates advanced, certified capabilities from the ICAI into the firm's core advisory services:

  • Global Reporting StandardsExpert capabilities in aligning complex corporate financials with global reporting standards. This ensures seamless cross-border financial transparency, robust compliance for large-scale enterprises, and structured guidance through complex transaction advisory.
  • Forensic & Governance AuditAdvanced skills in deep-dive financial investigations, corporate governance auditing, and structural risk assessment. This enables proactive vulnerability mapping to protect business assets, uncover systemic irregularities, and fortify internal financial controls.
  • AI-Driven Financial AutomationStrategic integration of cutting-edge automation, predictive tools, and artificial intelligence into mainstream financial workflows. This modern approach enhances audit accuracy, accelerates data extraction, and replaces manual tasks with real-time, data-driven business intelligence.

Lead — Accounts Associate

Mohammed Kareem

Mohammed Kareem handles the firm's day-to-day bookkeeping and reconciliation work, covering daily transaction entries, bank reconciliations (BRS), and ledger maintenance using accounting software including Tally, QuickBooks, and Zoho Books.

He verifies vendor invoices, manages voucher archives, and tracks accounts payable and receivable ageing. During data entry, he applies statutory compliance checks for TDS and GST and assists with payroll logging. He also resolves missing document queries with clients, maintains ledger accuracy, and organises working papers to support period-end closing and audit preparation.
